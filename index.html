YA COUNCIL OWES YOU MONEY FOR THIS

The Household Support Fund is a government grant. Your council has it. It pays for food, energy, essentials. You do not pay it back.

What to say when you call 📞

"Hi, I'm struggling to afford food right now. Can I apply for the Household Support Fund or any emergency food assistance please?"

That's it. They'll tell you what's available. Most councils can help same day.
